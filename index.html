Accountability Rules
Define your standards for follow-up speed, pipeline movement, and data entry — The Hub flags reps who fall behind and notifies the right people automatically
CRM Stage Rules
Rep Activity Rules
Notification Routing
Compliance Matrix
Each rule defines when a rep gets flagged for a lead that has stalled or skipped a required action in that stage, plus the grace window before the manager is escalated. These rules keep your pipeline data accurate and your funnel metrics trustworthy.
Rule 1
Unscheduled Lead
Stage: Lead — Lead has arrived and been assigned, but no appointment booked
ⓘ Rep notification: "Qualified lead not scheduled — book an appointment." Valid exit: lead moves to Appointment Scheduled.
Rule 2
Completed Appointment — No Disposition
Stage: Appointment Scheduled — Appointment ran but lead wasn't moved to Estimate or Lost
ⓘ Rep notification: "Completed appointment with no disposition — move to Estimate or mark Lost." Valid exits: Estimate or Lost.
Rule 3
Estimate Aging
Stage: Estimate — Estimate exists but no presentation scheduled within SLA
ⓘ Rep notification: "Estimate over 7 days — schedule the presentation." Valid exit: lead moves to Presentation.
Rule 4
Presentation Aging
Stage: Presentation — Estimate presented but no outcome recorded within SLA
ⓘ Rep notification: "Presentation over 7 days — record the outcome or move to Follow-up." Valid exits: Won, Lost, or Follow-up.
Rule 5
Follow-up Aging & Handoff
Stage: Follow-up — Lead has been nurturing too long without outcome, hands off to inside sales
ⓘ Rep notification: "Follow-up exceeded 14 days — handing off to inside sales." Valid exits: Won, Lost, or transferred to Inside Sales.
Manager Pattern Alert Thresholds
These fire to the manager independently of individual rep flags — they catch systemic non-compliance before it shows up in close rates.
flagged leads in a single stage
% overall compliance

Speed-to-Lead SLA

Estimate Delivery Rate

CRM Activity Minimum (Ghost Rep Rule)
Catches reps who are disengaged before it shows up in close rates — flagged to managers, not the rep
Not every breach should reach the CEO. Configure an escalation ladder so reps get a reminder first, managers step in if it's not fixed, and only repeat or serious patterns go to the top.
Escalation Ladder
Level 1
Warning — Rep only
First occurrence, within grace period. Tone: reminder, not accusation.
Level 2
Alert — Rep + Sales Manager
Grace period expired, first breach. Tone: action required.
Level 3
Escalation — Sales Manager + CEO
Second consecutive breach, or broken 3+ times in 30 days. Tone: pattern identified, needs intervention.
